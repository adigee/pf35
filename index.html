I am here to design and define.

I work at the overlap of product strategy, user research, interaction design, and visual craft — with a particular fondness for the 0→1 problem, the moment before the product exists.

I believe good design is argued for, not just executed. The best outcomes come from designers who can speak business and researchers who can speak design.

I am a business oriented product designer and digital storyteller.

I have 10+ years of experience across B2B and B2C, in startups and consulting.
