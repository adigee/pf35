Hi I’m Adi 👋

When I moved to Lisbon, my first landlord picked me up at the airport. I still remember that day, she even had a placard with my name!

After living with João for 5 years, I started to go to his hometown for Christmas. Living abroad, away from home, is an intense experience and landlords play a big role in it.

I can help HousingAnywhere build that trust.

The booking is where a tenancy starts, not where the work ends. Move-in prep, rent payments, a message to the other side, a problem to fix, an extension to agree. That’s where tenants and landlords decide whether they trust the platform.

I sit on both sides of this. As a tenant I know the feeling of not knowing what happens next. As a landlord I know what it takes to chase a payment or a reply. Each side’s confidence depends on the other’s, so the experience has to work for both.

In a mobile app these moments need to feel simple and reliable. I’d use qual discovery and behavioural data to find what actually causes friction, and design for that, not for opinions.

That’s what I’d bring to HousingAnywhere
