Hi I’m Adi 👋

When I moved to Lisbon, my first landlord came to the airport with a placard with my name!

My next landlord and flatmate was João. By the end of 5 years, I had visited João’s hometown for Christmas and he had visited Bombay and watched a Bollywood film with my mum! 🕺

After a decade of renting, now I am on the other side, as a landlord subletting a room in a flat. The mindset has shifted but the dynamics remain the same.

Living abroad is an intense, exhilarating experience, and a landlord plays a big role in it. Trust is fundamental to building a fruitful relationship for both sides. I can help HousingAnywhere build that trust.
