3 · Same mark on the OG card

Answer to "would the favicon also be used for the OG": the mark, yes — the file, no. OG needs a 1200×630 raster card (PNG, ≤300KB for WhatsApp); the favicon's exact geometry anchors it so tab, share card and avatar tell one story. Two directions below, shown at 50% scale, following the theme toggle. Type is Manrope 800 — name ≥90px at full size so it survives Slack/Discord's ~400px render.
