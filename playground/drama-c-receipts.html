Role

Team
Team of 3, embedded from Cocoon

Introduction

Insurance providers are aware that the two primary moments when a customer evaluates them are when they purchase the insurance and when they submit a claim.

Tranquilidade is an old-school insurance company in Portugal and was our client for this project. Our stakeholders were keen to improve the customer experience of submitting a claim, and they wanted to do it through a mobile app.

My role

I was embedded in a team of three from Cocoon on this digital transformation project. My role was to conceptualise and design the resulting mobile experience.

I was involved with onsite UX research, led the design of the solution, and moderated usability testing sessions.

Our objective

Going through a car accident is an emotionally stressful event with significant life and financial impact. To help customers through this trying time, we wanted to dramatically simplify the process to submit a claim.

Design iteration 3

Due to a healthy tension between the business analyst and me on the approach, we returned to the data to identify whether there were any other optimisations we could introduce.

By inferring the accident typology based on how the vehicles had impacted each other, we were able to detect over 90% of the accident types — allowing us to skip over all the remaining questions.

Reflections

Designing the DAD for Tranquilidade was a milestone project in my career, with insightful lessons for my design practice. Towards the end, we all truly felt like we had co-created this solution!
