Text highlighter — end-cap treatments →

2026-08-03

Diagnoses why the shipped highlighter marker (components/text_highlight.js) grows an odd dark smear at its trailing edge on longer phrases — the darker "pen-tip" cap is baked into the same stretchy path as the body, so it scales with phrase width instead of staying a constant size. Six side-by-side treatments: A current (bug reproduced), B no cap, B2 no cap with the right edge mirrored to match the left's natural taper, C soft gradient fade, D mirrored symmetric ends, and E a fixed-size end cap decoupled from the stretch. Theme, phrase-length, hover-state and cap-darkness dials to compare live, plus a section on line-wrap behavior. B2 was chosen and shipped in PR #114.
