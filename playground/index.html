A sandbox for feeling interactions, motion, and layout live in the browser before any production code exists.

This lives on the playground branch, which is never merged into main — nothing here ships to the production site. Each entry is a self-contained prototype kept as a durable reference. When an exploration wins, it's rebuilt cleanly on its own feature branch and PR'd to main.

Dark-mode image shadow →

2026-07-23

Case-study image shadows are pure black, so they vanish on the near-black dark bg but float on the light peach bg — a jarring mismatch when you flip themes. Dark and light shown side-by-side with a live treatment switch: Current (the bug), A · flush + hairline (no shadow, theme-aware border), and B · theme-aware lift (black drop shadow in light, soft shadow + light rim in dark). Dial-kit tunes the dark-mode rim / shadow, Copy CSS bakes the winner.

Rail hand-off — choreography tuner →

2026-07-22

The chosen rail hand-off, choreographed element-by-element. The eyebrow is the thread that connects the two pages: incoming, the page drops away to just the eyebrow which flies to its case-study slot; outgoing, the page zooms out and folds back into the homepage card (image + eyebrow). Full dial-kit per direction — delay / duration / distance / scale / easing, plus speed, scrub, reduced-motion and Copy config.
