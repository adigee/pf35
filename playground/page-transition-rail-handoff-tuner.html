Lockers PUDO

Timeline
2024–25
Role
Lead Product Designer
Team
Growth squad

Context

A new locker-based pick-up/drop-off use-case was cancelling at ~70%. Continuous discovery was set up to find where the flow was breaking and why.

Approach

Rather than a single redesign, the team ran tight build–measure–learn loops, shipping one hypothesis at a time and watching the cancellation curve respond.

Design

The booking flow was rebuilt around the two moments that mattered most: choosing a locker and confirming the drop. Everything else was stripped back.

Outcome

Cancellation fell from ~70% toward 15%, proving product–market fit for the new use-case.
