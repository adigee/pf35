Opportunity map: 4 directions

Demand-side tree, fully expanded. Every option applies your notes: no outlines on boxes, no heavy chips, generous padding (18–22px inside, 72px between columns). Throwaway page, the live map is untouched.

Option A

Soft tint

Closest to today, just calmer. Borderless pastel cards, type shown as small coloured text instead of a pill, hairline curved connectors. Safest swap; keeps the FigJam colour language you already use.

Option B

Lifted cards

Neutral white cards with a soft shadow (think Linear / Notion / Whimsical). Type is just a small coloured dot + label. Colour is nearly gone, so the highlighted solution can really pop. Most "product UI", least "diagram".

Option C

Lanes (OST-style)

Borrowed from how Teresa Torres draws opportunity solution trees: the level is named once in a column header, so no tag on every box. Boxes become pure content; connectors take the colour of the level they lead into, so the tree reads left to right at a glance.

Option D

Editorial outline

Boxes disappear. Text sits straight on the page with a coloured dot and fine connectors; only the highlighted solution gets a filled card. Most portfolio-like and calm, but nodes are less obviously tappable, so we'd lean on hover states and a chevron.
